# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Busy adults learning one new language (English, Spanish, German or French) in short daily sessions. They open the app during a break or in the evening, on a laptop or phone, often with headphones, and want to speak for about ten minutes with a patient AI tutor without planning anything themselves. They arrive with a native language and a vague goal ("travel", "talk to my in-laws", "work meetings").

## Product Purpose
An always-available spoken tutor. Onboarding captures the target language, the native language and how the learner likes to learn; an agent builds a long-term plan and, at every login, prepares the next ten-minute lesson from where the learner stands. The lesson is a live voice conversation that ticks off concrete objectives. Success: the learner comes back tomorrow because the last session felt doable, personal and noticeably useful.

## Brand Personality
Playful, encouraging, warm. Speaks like a friendly coach, never like a school or a chatbot. Celebrates small wins specifically and briefly. Grown-up playful: confident color and bouncy-but-not-silly moments, without cartoon overload.

## Anti-references
- Childish or cartoon overload: no mascots on every screen, no kid-app visuals, no confetti storms. This is for adults.
- A Duolingo clone: inspired by its friendliness and chunky affordances, but no owl-green branding, no copied streak/XP mechanics or layouts.
- The current dark editorial "AI tool" wizard feel.

## Design Principles
1. **Speaking is the product.** Every screen moves the learner toward the next spoken lesson; the live lesson screen gives the voice conversation centre stage.
2. **Progress you can feel.** Show the path and the objectives being ticked in real time, in plain words the learner recognises from the conversation.
3. **Zero planning burden.** The learner answers a few friendly questions once; afterwards it is always one clear "start" action.
4. **Encourage, never judge.** Copy and feedback praise effort, frame mistakes as next steps, and never shame missed days.
5. **Calm under the hood.** Connection states, mic permissions and errors are explained kindly with a clear way forward.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard access throughout. Honour `prefers-reduced-motion`. Color is never the only signal: completed objectives also use a check icon and text. Live transcript is visible for learners who prefer reading along or have hearing difficulty. Large tap targets for phone use.
