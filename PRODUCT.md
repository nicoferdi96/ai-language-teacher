# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Adults who want to learn a language (Spanish first) on their own terms. The
defining user is someone who does not want to spend a fixed hour with a human
tutor after a full day of work: they want flexibility over hours, lesson length,
and teaching style. They use the product at home, often in the evening, with
variable energy and limited patience for friction. The job to be done: make
steady progress in a language without a scheduling commitment.

The onboarding wizard is their first contact with the product. The task on every
wizard screen is to answer one short question so the teaching assistant can
shape a learning plan around them.

## Product Purpose

AI Language Teacher is an adaptive, always-available language learning platform.
It builds a personal learning plan that adapts to the learner and is there 24
hours a day, with no time commitment. It aims to replace both gamified apps and
paid human tutors at a fraction of the cost (around €20/month).

The onboarding wizard exists to introduce the teaching assistant and capture how
the learner wants to learn and their ideal lesson length. Those answers seed the
adaptive plan. Success: the learner finishes onboarding feeling understood and
motivated to begin, not processed by a form.

## Brand Personality

Calm, encouraging, patient. A tutor who never rushes you. Three words: calm,
encouraging, steady. The voice is warm but never childish, supportive, and
plain-spoken: it talks like a real teacher having a quiet conversation. The
emotional goal is ease and low pressure. The learner should feel in control of
their own time, because flexibility is the product.

## Anti-references

Not cluttered and not corporate. No dense multi-field forms, no legalese, no
cold enterprise tone, no wall of inputs. Nothing on screen should compete with
the single question being asked. The onboarding must never feel like filling out
paperwork.

## Design Principles

- **One thing at a time.** Never show more than the single question that
  matters now. The wizard is paced, not packed.
- **The learner sets the pace.** Flexibility is the product. The interface must
  never pressure, rush, or guilt the learner into moving faster.
- **Speak like a patient tutor.** Copy is warm, encouraging, and plain. The
  assistant has a voice and uses it; it does not bark form labels.
- **Calm over clever.** Restraint wins. Quiet confidence, generous space,
  nothing decorative that distracts from the question.
- **Earn trust early.** Onboarding stands in for a human teacher's first
  lesson. It should feel personal and attentive, so the learner believes the
  plan that follows will fit them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA, with extras. AA contrast across the dark editorial palette,
visible keyboard focus on every interactive element, and full keyboard
operability. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` by reducing or removing transitions.
Color-blind-safe: never rely on color alone to convey state (errors, focus,
selection also use text, shape, or weight).
